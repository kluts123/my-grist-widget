Engineering management principles

CINCOMETRIC is being shaped by practical lessons from project management, project controls, engineering management and industrial project delivery.

1

Start with scope and design maturity

Freeze scope only when the front-end loading and key design inputs are sufficiently mature. Immature specifications create rework and change later.

2

Build the right team early

Core project roles need clear ownership and early involvement. Fragmented responsibilities create delays and gaps.

3

Manage contracts proactively

Contracts should be used as management tools. Deviations, interfaces and obligations need active monitoring before they become problems.

4

Bring procurement in early

Procurement and vendor considerations belong in the project from the early phases, with appropriate supplier oversight.

5

Make ownership and interfaces explicit

Clear responsibilities and interfaces between disciplines, vendors, project teams and operations reduce gaps, overlap and rework.

6

Treat data, documents and communication as project assets

Information needs defined ownership, disciplined change processes and a common source of truth.

7

Safety and operations belong in the design

HSE, operations and maintenance should influence engineering and design decisions early rather than becoming late-stage stakeholders.

8

Construction and commissioning need clear accountability

Build and start-up have different responsibilities, but they need to remain aligned through structured handover and system tracking.
